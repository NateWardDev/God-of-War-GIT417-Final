GODS, WARRIORS & LEGENDS

KRATOS

A former Spartan warrior burdened by his past, Kratos tries to live a quiet life in the Norse realms. Guarded and stoic, he must teach his son to survive while keeping his true nature hidden from the gods.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Leviathan Axe • Guardian Shield • Blades of Chaos

ATREUS

Kratos's son, eager to learn and understand his identity. Equipped with sharp archery skills and a compassionate heart, Atreus plays a vital role in navigating the complex web of Norse deities and prophecies.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Talon Bow • Magic Summoning • Runic Arts

FREYA

Freya is a powerful goddess and former Valkyrie queen with deep ties to the realms and their ancient magic. Intelligent, fierce, and deeply connected to those she loves, she is both a formidable warrior and an important ally.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Mardöll (Sword) • Valkyrie Bow • Seiðr Magic

THOR

The Aesir God of Thunder, known for his unmatched strength and brutal temper. Driven by loyalty to Odin and the loss of his sons, Thor is a devastating force sent to break Kratos and Atreus.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Mjölnir (Hammer) • Lightning Control

BALDUR

An Aesir god driven mad by his inability to feel physical sensation. Relentless and seemingly impervious to pain, Baldur tracks Kratos down to the ends of the realm with terrifying aggression.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Hand-to-Hand Combat • Elemental Magic

ODIN

The Allfather and ruler of Asgard. Obsessed with preventing his foretold demise, Odin manipulates events across the Nine Realms through deception, intellect, and sheer psychological control.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Gungnir (Spear) • Ancient Runes • Raven Spies

BROK & SINDRI

The legendary Huldra brothers and master blacksmiths responsible for crafting some of the finest weapons in the realms, including Kratos's Leviathan Axe and Mjölnir itself.

SIGNATURE WEAPONS:

Blacksmith Hammers • Mystical Portals
