A NEW WORLD. AN OLD GOD.

After leaving his violent past behind, Kratos builds a new life in the Norse world with his son, Atreus. What begins as a journey to honor a loved one draws them into a world of gods, monsters, and ancient secrets. Together, they must confront their past and decide what kind of future they will create.
